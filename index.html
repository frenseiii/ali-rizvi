Why me, for this problem

"I've sold in a high-touch market. Relationships decide revenue, and CRMs only know what someone typed in."

I sold enterprise account-planning software built on Salesforce, closing Siemens, Medtronic and Cummins. I watched deals live and die on relationships the tools never saw.

Then I built Frensei PhD Peer: communication intelligence that catches early drift between PhD students and supervisors, in a field where up to 70% of candidates never finish. It worked, and it drew strategic interest from leading research-tech companies, including Digital Science.

ClawbackVault applies the same idea where it costs the most: client relationships. I took a sabbatical from teaching, raised six figures and moved to Canada to build it full time.

Building now

ClawbackVault: the relationship brain for high-touch businesses

High-touch businesses set a high bar before the sale, then struggle to keep that warmth once the client is won. ClawbackVault discovers a firm's own best practices from its client conversations, scales them across the team with a morning list, drafts and automatic chasing, and keeps learning. People and AI agents can both work from the same brain. A person always presses send.

  • Discover: what actually keeps this firm's clients
  • Scale: every person, and every AI agent, works that way
  • Trust: read-only, approved clients only, masked before any AI
